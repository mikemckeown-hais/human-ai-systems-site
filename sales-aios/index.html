The platform advantage

One place to work, with everything else connected

Most sales teams use a long list of platforms: CRM, call recording, outbound, sales intelligence, quoting, content and a good few spreadsheets. Each one takes time to learn, people use them differently, and few get the full value from any of them.

With a Sales AI Operating System the AI sits at the centre and connects to the rest. Reps can still go into any platform when they want to. Most of the time they don’t need to. They tell the AI what happened and what they want, and it does the updating.

One instruction after a customer meeting

“Write up this customer meeting, capture the actions, draft the follow-up email, update the CRM and the knowledge base, and start a proposal. I think it has moved to qualification, and I’d put it at 80% to close this quarter.”

One way of working to learn

New starters learn one way of working instead of eight tools. The other platforms become systems of record that the AI keeps up to date, so the data in them is better too.

Often, less spent on software

Most meeting platforms already record and transcribe, and in person a phone’s voice memo app does the job, so a separate AI note-taker may not be needed. Premium tiers bought for their AI features may not be either. Some teams move to a lower-cost platform, with the AI helping set it up and move the data.

More from the platforms you keep

The AI uses each platform more fully than most people have time to, and joins up information that today sits in separate tools.

Common questions

What sales leaders usually ask

Is this more software we have to buy?

No. It runs on the AI platform your company already pays for, such as Microsoft Copilot, Claude or ChatGPT, and connects to the systems you already have. What you pay for is setting it up for your sales team, training them and, if you want it, ongoing support.

Our IT team looks after AI. Isn’t this their job?

IT looks after the tool: which AI platform you use, security, access and which connections are approved. We work with them and within those decisions, using the AI they have already approved. If you haven’t chosen a platform yet, we can suggest one and help your IT team through their assessment.

Setting the tool up for sales is a different job. Think of Excel. IT provides it, but the finance team builds the models, the pivot tables and the macros, because only finance knows how the numbers need to work. A Sales AI Operating System is the sales team’s version of that, and it needs people who know how sales works. One governed setup for the whole team is also easier for IT to approve and support than everyone building their own.

We already have a CRM and a call recording tool. Why add this?

It builds on them rather than replacing them. Your CRM and call recording tools show you what is happening in the pipeline. This does the rep’s preparation, follow-up and drafting work, using the same information, and keeps the CRM more up to date as a result.

Could we build it ourselves?

Yes. Doing it well takes well over 100 hours, plus the know-how to make it complete and keep it working. We have a proven, repeatable method, so your people spend their time on the knowledge only they have, and the team gets the benefit in weeks.

Will the reps use it?

That is what the pilot and the launch are for. Four or five reps test it first and become its champions. The launch is hands-on, on people’s own accounts, with skills for each role. Then we track how many people use it each week, and what is getting used.

What about customer data?

Customer data never goes into the setup itself. It stays in your systems and reaches the AI only through your own approved connections when someone runs a task. If we need temporary access to install it for you, that is covered by a data processing agreement and a confidentiality agreement.

How will we know it has worked?

Before launch we time two or three tasks your reps do often, such as meeting preparation or a first-draft proposal. We time them again a few weeks after launch. Alongside that you can see adoption across the team, proposal turnaround and, over time, win rates.

The morning brief

What it is

A short briefing waiting for each rep before the day starts, put together overnight from their email, calendar, CRM and tasks.

What it does for you

  • The day starts on the things that move deals forward, not on the inbox.
  • Replies that need sending are already drafted in your voice, ready to check and send.
  • Nothing slips. Quiet deals, overdue follow-ups and tasks due are flagged every day.

How it works

A scheduled task runs overnight. It reads the rep’s inbox and calendar, checks the CRM for deals that have gone quiet or have close dates coming up, and pulls in the task list. It prepares a brief for each meeting and drafts a reply to each important email. It uses your sales method from the knowledge base to decide what matters most, and shows the result as a simple dashboard.

  • Outlook or Gmail
  • Calendar
  • Salesforce, HubSpot or Dynamics
  • Task list
  • Knowledge base
  • Dashboard

Meeting and call preparation

What it is

A one-page brief for every external meeting or call: who is attending, what has happened on the account, what they are likely to care about, what to lead with and what to ask.

What it does for you

  • Every rep walks in as well prepared as your best rep.
  • The hours spent researching each week come back.
  • Better questions mean better discovery, and better discovery wins more deals.

How it works

Before each meeting the AI gathers the account’s history from the CRM, previous emails, meeting write-ups and proposals. For new contacts it researches the company and recent news, and reads the person’s LinkedIn profile if the rep adds it. It checks your products and case studies in the knowledge base to suggest what to lead with, and your playbook for the questions to ask. If you record calls with Gong or a similar tool, it adds what the customer said last time.

  • CRM
  • Email and calendar
  • Web research
  • LinkedIn profiles
  • Gong, Teams or Zoom recordings
  • Knowledge base

Meeting write-up, actions and CRM update

What it is

The meeting turned into notes, decisions, actions and updates within minutes, whether it was a video call, a phone call or a meeting in person.

What it does for you

  • No more evenings writing up notes and updating the CRM.
  • The CRM stays accurate, because keeping it up to date takes seconds.
  • Every commitment made to a customer is captured and tracked, so it gets done.

How it works

The rep gives it whatever they have: a recording or transcript from Teams, Zoom or Google Meet, a voice memo recorded on the way out of a customer’s office, or a few rough notes. The AI writes it up in your format, fills in your qualification fields, lists the actions, drafts the follow-up email and updates the CRM, adding the rep’s own judgement, such as “move this to qualification, 80% likely to close this quarter”. Anything worth keeping about the account goes into the knowledge base, so the next brief is better.

  • Teams, Zoom or Meet transcripts
  • Phone voice memos

Proposals, quotes and RFPs

What it is

First drafts of proposals and RFP responses in your format, built from your templates, approved answers and the customer’s own words.

What it does for you

  • Days of drafting become hours of checking. At Verimatrix, proposal workload and turnaround fell by 50 to 60%.
  • Every proposal uses your best material and current product information.
  • Faster, better tailored proposals win more often.

How it works

The AI starts from the meeting write-ups and the customer’s stated priorities, then draws on the knowledge base: templates, past winning proposals, product descriptions, pricing rules and the approved RFP answer library. It follows your proposal method and house style, and files the draft where your team keeps them. Your experts review it and decide. Quotes follow once your CRM or quoting tool is connected. The same material can also become a rich proposal page with video and narration.

Rich proposal pages and presentations

What it is

Proposals that are more than documents: a private web page for each customer with their priorities, an interactive view of the solution, a short video and narration, alongside decks tailored to the customer.

What it does for you

  • You stand out from competitors sending PDFs.
  • It is easy for your champion to share inside their company, so they can sell for you when you’re not in the room.
  • It takes an afternoon. Until recently this needed a design agency and weeks of work.

How it works

The AI builds the page from the same material as the written proposal, on brand, from simple direction such as “lead with their delivery problem and keep it short”. Video and voice can be the rep’s own recordings, or an AI avatar and an AI clone of the rep’s voice made with tools such as HeyGen and ElevenLabs, depending on the rep’s preference and time. Decks start from your standard deck, adapted to the customer, with speaker notes.

  • Knowledge base and brand guidelines
  • Meeting write-ups
  • HeyGen, ElevenLabs or your own recordings
  • Your website

Pipeline reviews

What it is

A weekly review of the pipeline that reads the whole picture, not just the stage totals.

What it does for you

  • You know where the risk is before the forecast call.
  • Less time in spreadsheets and more time coaching on the deals that matter.
  • Decisions based on what customers said, not just what was typed into the CRM.

A scheduled task reads the CRM, recent meeting write-ups, emails and proposals for each open deal, and compares them with your sales method and targets. Account research

What it is

A short, useful profile of any target company: what they do, recent news, likely priorities, who to talk to and why your offer fits.

What it does for you

  • Research that took an hour takes minutes.
  • Every approach starts from a good reason to talk.
  • The same standard for every account, whoever does it.

How it works

The AI researches the company on the web, reads its site and news, checks the CRM for any previous contact, and compares what it finds with your ideal customer profile and case studies in the knowledge base. Reps can give it a LinkedIn profile, or a lead list exported from Sales Navigator, to work through.

Personalised outreach

What it is

First emails and sequences written for each prospect from the research, in your voice, loaded into your outbound tool.

What it does for you

  • Personal messages at volume, without the generic feel.
  • More replies, because each message has a relevant reason to talk.
  • Your rules kept: who can be contacted, how often, and what is said.

How it works

Starting from a lead list from Sales Navigator, your CRM or a data provider, the AI researches each contact, writes the message and loads it into your outbound tool. It checks the CRM so nobody already in a conversation is contacted, and follows your rules on suppression and opt-outs. A person approves before anything is sent.

Deal risk

Early warning on deals at risk, with the reason and a suggested action.
