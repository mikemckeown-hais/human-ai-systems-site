What I can do for you

Before the deal, after it, and on the board

Before you buy

  • Screening targets and reviewing pitch decks
  • Technology due diligence: product, architecture, engineering capability, scalability and technical debt
  • Cyber and data risk
  • AI exposure: whether AI threatens the business model or can lift its margins, and how credible management's AI plans are
  • The strength of the technology and commercial teams

After you buy

  • Separation from a parent's systems, or integration of an acquired team
  • The first 100 days: CRM, lead-to-cash and the operating model, including offshoring
  • Value creation through digital and AI, measured in win rates, productivity and cost to serve
  • AI governance the board can sign off

On the board

  • Independent director on companies you acquire
  • Governance ready for growth, exit or a long hold
  • CEO succession, organisation design and culture
  • Oversight of technology, cyber and AI risk
Track record

Deal and value-creation experience

Cisco

Two multi-billion-pound acquisitions

I was a member of the small pre-acquisition team, working under NDA, for Cisco's purchase of NDS, the pay-TV technology company based in the UK and Israel. After completion I led the integration of the combined business development and presales organisations. I also worked on the integration of Tandberg, the Norwegian video communications company.

Alongside the deal work, I ran Cisco's EMEA education and local government business, over £300M a year, and grew it by 20 per cent.

Synamedia, Permira

A private equity carve-out

In 2018 I was part of the team that handled the sale of Cisco's service provider video business to Permira, creating Synamedia, an independent company of 3,500 people. I then led sales and service operations through the separation and helped set the new company up to stand on its own.

What I led

The new company's global sales operations function, with standardised KPIs and board-level reporting.

Its own CRM, as business lead. We moved off Cisco's systems early, saving millions in charges for using them.

The move of a substantial part of engineering and support to India. It was a difficult change for the people affected. It cut annual costs by millions while keeping service to customers running.

Verimatrix, EU-listed cybersecurity company

AI value creation, with governance the board approved

The CEO asked me to lead AI transformation across a nine-country business. I wrote the Responsible AI framework, aligned to the EU AI Act, GDPR and DORA, took it to the board for approval, and measured the results in business terms.

Results

~10% better win rates in the SaaS business

~1 hour a day back for every engineer

~30% fewer routine support tickets

Gravitas Tech and Solutions, US AI start-up, advisory board

The founder's side of a funding round

I advise the founders on strategy and fundraising: investor readiness, valuation and dilution, and the choice between SAFEs and priced equity. I helped shape an investor-grade financial model and challenged its assumptions on capital efficiency, linking each funding step to product, user-growth and commercial milestones.

How I work

As a retained operating partner, a few hours a week or by the deal: desk reviews, management meetings and clear written findings. If both sides want it, I join the board of a company you acquire as an independent director. I am based in the Cotswolds and happy to travel.

How I handle conflicts

I declare my interests at every board. I run an AI consultancy, and I never sell its services to a board I sit on. As an elected councillor, I do not take roles where the organisation's business comes before my council.
