Non-executive director profile

Commercial and public-service leadership, with deep experience of technology and AI.

I'm a non-executive director and charity trustee, a Cabinet Member at Cotswold District Council, and the founder of Human-AI Systems. Over 25 years I have run commercial businesses, taken decisions in public, and helped boards oversee change, including the technology and AI that now sit behind most of it.

In short

What I bring to a board

Strategy, deals and commercial judgement. I ran and grew an EMEA public sector business at Cisco, worked on the due diligence and integration of two multi-billion-dollar acquisitions, helped create Synamedia through a private equity carve-out, and advise a US start-up on raising venture capital. I read a plan through its numbers, its risks and its people.

Governance and constructive challenge. I sit on a charity board, chair a council oversight board and co-founded a community benefit society. I test proposals hard while leaving ownership with the executive.

Public accountability. As an elected Cabinet Member I take decisions in public, answer to scrutiny, work with partners across the county and live within statutory duties and a fixed budget.

Technology and AI, in business terms. This is my specialism. I help a board see what a technology investment will cost, what could go wrong and what value it should deliver, so it can decide with confidence.

People and culture. I have led teams across Europe and Asia and worked across more than a dozen countries. I also mentor emerging leaders on the Stepping Up programme.

Board work in practice

Examples

Gloucestershire Rural Community Council, trustee and director

Strengthening a major investment decision

Soon after I joined the board, trustees considered a proposal for a new digital platform to bring together the charity's volunteer, activity and impact data. It was a significant investment for a small charity. The board asked for more work before deciding, and I joined a working group with the chief executive, the head of finance, the project sponsor and a fellow trustee to strengthen it.

What the revised proposal gained

A clearer, itemised budget and business case.

Data protection, security and oversight of AI designed in from the start, including independent security testing before launch.

Documentation and version control, so the platform does not depend on any one person.

Integration with the charity's existing systems, so staff record information once.

I then set out the further conditions to meet before live personal data goes onto the platform. My role was to help the board ask the right questions and to give the executive team a clear set of requirements. The team owned the proposal and did the work.

Cotswold District Council, Cabinet Member for Digital and Climate

Cabinet leadership and chairing the AI Board

With the council's Chief Information Officer, I co-authored the Responsible AI Policy and the AI Strategy, both adopted by Cabinet. I pressed for an AI Board to oversee them, and I chair it. It meets quarterly and works much like a board committee: it scrutinises proposals to use AI, reviews progress against the strategy and keeps adoption responsible. It also set up an AI Champions programme, which now drives adoption across the council.

Results

40%+ faster decisions on planning applications, using an AI planning system that supports planning officers

~8 weeks of officer time saved analysing Local Plan consultation responses

~250 officers and members using Microsoft Copilot under the policy

Beyond digital, I lead the council's climate programme. The council has moved more than 60 places up the national Climate Action Scorecard, and I chaired Climate Leadership Gloucestershire, bringing the county's public sector partners together on net zero.

Cisco and Synamedia

Acquisitions, a carve-out and growth

At Cisco I ran an EMEA public sector business of over £300M and grew it by 20 per cent. I was also part of the teams that carried out due diligence on, and then integrated, two multi-billion-dollar acquisitions: Tandberg, the Norwegian video communications company, and NDS, the pay-TV technology company based in the UK and Israel.

In 2018 I was part of the team that handled the sale of Cisco's service provider video business to Permira, creating Synamedia, a newly independent company of 3,500 people. I then helped separate it from Cisco and set it up to stand on its own.

What I led at Synamedia

The commercial and operational processes of the new company.

The new company's own CRM, as business lead. We moved off Cisco's systems early, saving millions in charges for using them.

The move of a substantial part of engineering and support to India. It was a difficult change for the people affected. It cut annual costs by millions while service to customers carried on.

Gravitas Tech and Solutions, US AI start-up, advisory board

Advising founders on raising venture capital

I advise the founders on strategy and fundraising. That has covered investor readiness for a friends-and-family round and a planned seed round, funding strategy, valuation and dilution, and the choice between SAFEs and priced equity, with a later Series A in mind.

I helped shape an investor-grade financial model covering revenue scenarios, pricing, operating and AI infrastructure costs, customer acquisition cost, cash burn and runway. I also challenged the assumptions on capital efficiency, and linked each funding step to product, user-growth and commercial milestones.

Verimatrix, EU-listed cybersecurity company

Board-approved AI governance that paid its way

The CEO asked me to lead AI transformation across a nine-country business. I wrote the Responsible AI framework, aligned to the EU AI Act, GDPR and DORA, and took it to the board for approval. A steering group reported progress to the executive committee, and the results were measured in business terms.

Results

~10% better win rates in the SaaS business

~1 hour a day back for every engineer

~30% fewer routine support tickets
