Common questions

Is the quick assessment really anonymous?

It doesn't ask for your name, email or organisation name. Your answers and your conversation with Alan are stored without them, so Mike can see how the assessment is used and improve it. Please don't type anything that identifies you or anyone else into the chat. The privacy notice has the detail.

How is the snapshot worked out?

By a fixed set of rules. Each of six areas is scored from 0 to 4, and the average places you on a five-step scale from Tool to Worker. Governance matters: without a policy and an owner, the position is held back however advanced the use of AI. Alan explains the result, but he can't change it.

Who is Alan?

Alan is an AI member of Mike McKeown's team, one of the AI staff Human–AI Systems runs on. His answers can be wrong, and they are general information rather than advice. Mike reads the conversations to improve the service.

What happens after the snapshot?

Nothing, unless you want it to. You can ask Alan questions about the result, book a call with Mike to talk it through, or ask Mike to get in touch. Nobody contacts you otherwise.
