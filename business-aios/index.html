Business AI Operating System

Get far more from AI, for you and your whole team

Most people use AI as a clever assistant that has to be briefed from scratch for every task. A Business AI Operating System changes that. It gives the AI you already pay for the knowledge of your business, the skills to do your team’s real work, and routines that run without being asked. The result is AI that works like part of the team.

What it is Watch it in action
The idea

Your own operating system for working with AI

A computer is only useful once it has an operating system. AI is much the same. On its own it is capable but generic. It doesn’t know your customers, your products, your policies or the way your team likes things done, so every task starts with explaining all of that again.

A Business AI Operating System is the layer that fixes this. It sits on the AI platform you already use and turns it into something that knows how your organisation works and does the work the way you would.

It runs in your own accounts, with your files in your own cloud storage, and people stay in charge of anything that leaves the business.

Knowledge

What your organisation knows, in one place the AI can draw on: customers, products, policies and how you do things.

Skills

How your team does its work, written down so the AI does it consistently: proposals, reports, follow-ups, briefings.

Routines and dashboards

Work that runs on a schedule by itself, such as a morning brief or a weekly pipeline review, with the results where you can see them.

Connections

Links into your email, calendar, files and systems such as your CRM, so it works from real, current information.

What you end up with

More than a faster way to write emails

Hours back every week

The gathering, chasing and first drafts are done for you, so the time goes on decisions and on customers. How much comes back depends on the role. It is usually hours a week, and for some roles a full day. When our founder led AI adoption at Verimatrix, developers got back about an hour a day.

A team working from the same picture

Everyone draws on the same knowledge of the business, so the work is more consistent and of a higher standard.

AI use you can see and control

Instead of everyone experimenting on their own, there is one shared way of working, in your own accounts, that you can check, improve and build on.
