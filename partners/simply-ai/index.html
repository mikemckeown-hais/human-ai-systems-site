Human–AI Systems and Simply AI

Simply AI builds voice AI agents for telecoms providers and the businesses they serve. We build Business AI Operating Systems. Together we connect the two, so a business’s AI can take a call, follow it up and act on what it hears.

Two halves of one offer

What each of us brings

Simply AI

The voice and conversation platform

Voice agents that answer and make calls in 27 languages, and also work across web chat and email. They register on a hosted phone system like any other extension, so no numbers need to move, and the platform is white-label for the providers who resell it.

Human–AI Systems

The operating system behind the conversation

The knowledge, skills, routines and connections that let AI do real work for a business, built on the AI platform it already uses. We train its people to run it, with governance in place from the start.

For a business

What connecting them makes possible

Calls that know the business

The voice agent draws on the same knowledge as the rest of the team: customers, products and policies. What it hears goes back into the operating system, so whoever picks it up next starts from the full picture.

Work that can reach people

Partway through a task, the operating system can reach a customer or colleague by phone and bring the answer back. Staff away from a desk can speak to it instead of typing. People still decide what goes out in the business’s name.

Voice is not part of every AI operating system we build. It is one of the further connections a business can add when calls and messages are a real part of its work. See what else it can connect to.
