Three ways to work together

Pick the one that fits the client and the relationship. Many partners use more than one.

Who we are

Human–AI Systems

We help organisations move from scattered AI experiments to disciplined, everyday use. Adopting AI is mostly about how people and teams work, so we spend as much time on roles, governance and habits as on the technology.

Consulting

From first decisions to organisation-wide adoption

We work through a structured path: assess where AI can create value and where the risks sit, pilot it in real workflows with clear measures, scale what works across the organisation, and support leadership over the long term. Engagements range from a half-day leadership session to fractional Chief AI Officer support.

Our methodology combines Radar, Pilot, Scale for adoption with the Tool, Assistant, Worker model for how AI capability matures, and a practical approach to leading mixed human and AI teams.
